## Getting Started

### What is GrieferHub?

GrieferHub is a community-driven platform for tracking and reporting griefers in gaming communities. Whether you've encountered a toxic player, witnessed griefing, or want to check if someone has a history of disruptive behavior, GrieferHub provides the tools you need.

### Key Features

- **Public Intel Board**: Browse verified reports of griefers
- **Report Submission**: Submit evidence of griefing behavior
- **User Dashboard**: Track your submitted reports
- **Advanced Search**: Filter by game, severity, and status
- **Video Evidence**: Upload or link video proof

## Browsing Reports

### Accessing the Intel Board

The Intel Board is available at `/intel` and is accessible to everyone without an account.

**Features**:
- View all verified and pending reports
- Filter by game, status, and severity
- Search by username or description
- View detailed report pages with evidence

### Filtering Reports

Use the filter options to narrow down results:

1. **By Game**: Select from the dropdown menu
2. **By Status**:
   - Pending: Under review
   - Verified: Confirmed by moderators
   - Rejected: Insufficient evidence
   - Resolved: Action taken
3. **By Severity**:
   - Low: Minor infractions
   - Medium: Repeated issues
   - High: Serious violations
   - Critical: Severe or dangerous behavior

### Viewing Report Details

Click on any report to view:
- Full description
- Video/image evidence
- Submission date
- Current status
- Severity level
- Tags and categories
- Server information

## Creating an Account

### Why Create an Account?

With a free account, you can:
- Submit your own griefer reports
- Track your submission history
- Edit and manage your reports
- Build credibility in the community

### Registration Process

1. Navigate to `/register`
2. Provide:
   - Email address
   - Password (minimum 8 characters)
   - Display name
3. Click "Register"
4. You'll be automatically logged in

### Login

1. Go to `/login`
2. Enter your email and password
3. Click "Sign In"

**Tip**: Keep your credentials secure and don't share your account.

## Submitting Reports

### Before You Submit

**Checklist**:
- [ ] Have clear evidence (video/screenshot/link)
- [ ] Know the game and server information
- [ ] Can describe the incident clearly
- [ ] Understand the severity level

### Step-by-Step Submission

1. **Navigate to Submit Page** (`/submit`)
   - Must be logged in

2. **Fill Out Required Fields**:
   - **Griefer Username**: The player's in-game name
   - **Game**: Select or type the game name
   - **Description**: Detailed account of the incident
   - **Severity**: Choose appropriate level

3. **Add Evidence** (Optional but Recommended):
   - **Upload File**:
     - Images: Max 10MB (JPG, PNG, GIF, WEBP)
     - Videos: Max 100MB (MP4, WEBM, MOV, AVI)
   - **Or Provide URL**: Link to YouTube, Twitch, etc.

4. **Additional Information**:
   - **Server ID**: Server name or IP
   - **Tags**: Add relevant categories
   - **Alternative URL**: Additional evidence links

5. **Review and Submit**:
   - Check all information
   - Click "Submit Report"
   - You'll be redirected to your new report

### Evidence Guidelines

**Good Evidence**:
- Clear video showing the incident
- Screenshots with timestamps
- Multiple angles or perspectives
- Context showing unprovoked behavior

**What to Avoid**:
- Blurry or unclear footage
- Out-of-context clips
- Evidence that violates privacy
- Edited or manipulated content

### Severity Levels Explained

**Low**:
- Minor annoyances
- First-time offenses
- Minimal impact on gameplay

**Medium**:
- Repeated infractions
- Moderate disruption
- Pattern of negative behavior

**High**:
- Serious violations
- Significant impact on community
- Intentional griefing
- Harassment or toxic behavior

**Critical**:
- Extreme cases
- Threats of violence
- Doxxing attempts
- Coordinated attacks
- Exploits or hacking

## Managing Your Reports

### Accessing Your Dashboard

Navigate to `/dashboard` to view all your submitted reports.

### Dashboard Features

**Statistics Overview**:
- Total reports submitted
- Verified reports
- Reports under review
- Resolved cases

**Report List**:
- See all your submissions
- Current status of each report
- Quick actions (View, Edit, Delete)

### Editing Reports

You can edit your reports before they're verified:

1. Go to your dashboard
2. Click "Edit" on the report
3. Update any information
4. Click "Save Changes"

**Note**: Once a report is verified, you can no longer edit it. Contact a moderator if corrections are needed.

### Deleting Reports

To remove a report:

1. Go to your dashboard
2. Click "Delete" on the report
3. Confirm deletion

**Warning**: Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone.

## Understanding Report Statuses

### Status Flow

```
Pending → Verified → Resolved
   ↓
Rejected
```

### Status Definitions

**Pending**:
- Default status for new submissions
- Awaiting moderator review
- Can still be edited by submitter

**Verified**:
- Reviewed and confirmed by moderators
- Evidence deemed sufficient
- Visible on Intel Board
- Cannot be edited by submitter

**Rejected**:
- Insufficient evidence
- Duplicate report
- Violates submission guidelines
- Will not appear on Intel Board

**Resolved**:
- Action has been taken
- Case is closed
- Archived but still visible

### What Happens After Submission?

1. **Immediate**: Report is created with "Pending" status
2. **Within 24-48 hours**: Moderator reviews your report
3. **Review Complete**: Status changes to Verified or Rejected
4. **If Verified**: Report appears on Intel Board
5. **If Rejected**: You'll see rejection reason in dashboard

## Best Practices

### Effective Reporting

1. **Be Specific**: Include exact usernames, dates, and times
2. **Provide Context**: Explain what led to the incident
3. **Use Clear Evidence**: High-quality videos or screenshots
4. **Stay Objective**: Focus on facts, not emotions
5. **Follow Up**: Check your dashboard for status updates

### What Makes a Good Report?

**Example of a Good Report**:
```
Username: PlayerX123
Game: Minecraft
Server: mc.example.com
Description: On January 10, 2026 at approximately 3:00 PM EST,
PlayerX123 deliberately destroyed multiple builds in the spawn
area using TNT. The player had been warned by admins earlier
in the day but continued griefing. Video evidence shows the
entire incident including chat logs where the player admits
to the griefing.
Severity: High
Evidence: [Video upload showing timestamps and clear actions]
Tags: griefing, property-destruction, repeat-offender
```

### Community Guidelines

**DO**:
- Submit factual, evidence-based reports
- Use appropriate severity levels
- Respect other users' privacy
- Update reports if situation changes
- Be patient with the review process

**DON'T**:
- Submit false or misleading reports
- Use the platform for personal vendettas
- Harass or dox other users
- Submit duplicate reports
- Upload irrelevant content

## FAQ

### General Questions

**Q: Is GrieferHub free to use?**
A: Yes, all features are completely free.

**Q: Do I need an account to view reports?**
A: No, the Intel Board is public. You only need an account to submit reports.

**Q: How long does review take?**
A: Most reports are reviewed within 24-48 hours.

**Q: Can I appeal a rejected report?**
A: Contact a moderator with additional evidence or clarification.

### Submission Questions

**Q: What file formats are supported?**
A: Images (JPG, PNG, GIF, WEBP) and videos (MP4, WEBM, MOV, AVI).

**Q: What's the maximum file size?**
A: 10MB for images, 100MB for videos.

**Q: Can I submit multiple pieces of evidence?**
A: Currently, one upload per report, but you can add alternative URLs.

**Q: What if I don't have video evidence?**
A: Screenshots and detailed descriptions are also accepted, though video is preferred.

**Q: Can I submit reports for multiple games?**
A: Yes, each report is game-specific.

### Account Questions

**Q: How do I reset my password?**
A: Use the "Forgot Password" link on the login page.

**Q: Can I change my display name?**
A: Yes, in your account settings (coming in Phase 6).

**Q: Can I delete my account?**
A: Contact support to request account deletion.

**Q: Are my reports anonymous?**
A: Your display name is shown on reports you submit.

### Technical Questions

**Q: What browsers are supported?**
A: All modern browsers (Chrome, Firefox, Safari, Edge).

**Q: Is there a mobile app?**
A: Not yet, but the website is mobile-responsive. Mobile app planned for Phase 7.

**Q: Can I use the API?**
A: Yes, see [API_DOCUMENTATION.md](../API_DOCUMENTATION.md).

**Q: Is my data secure?**
A: Yes, we use industry-standard security practices. See [SECURITY.md](../SECURITY.md).
